THE RULER OF DESMONIA

Welcome to my kingdom

I am Queen G, the vampire queen of Desmonia. From the darkest corners of the kingdom, I rule over ghouls, ghosties, vampires, and all creatures of the night.

I founded this kingdom when I was running away from the humans. I stumbled upon a forest with Wysteria trees and found an underground cave system. From that moment I knew I had found my home. I built my kingdom and welcome in any creature of the night that wishes to join me.

When I'm not ruling my kingdom, you'll find me playing horror games, indie games, yapping about random things, and occasionally watching random videos.
